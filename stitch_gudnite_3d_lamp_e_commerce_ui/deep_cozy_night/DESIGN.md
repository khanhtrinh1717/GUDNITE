## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an immersive, atmospheric night-mode experience designed specifically for custom 3D-printed pixel lamps. It transports the user into a quiet, darkened bedroom illuminated solely by the warm, diffuse, 2700K radiance and pastel glow of bedside ambient objects. The experience resonates deeply with aesthetic desk curators, night owls, gamers, and cozy lo-fi lifestyle enthusiasts who prize physical tactile craftsmanship intertwined with digital pixel nostalgia.

The movement unifies **Nocturnal Tactile Glassmorphism** with **Luminous Pixel Geometry**. Deep, velvety purple-slate and obsidian tones form an infinite spatial depth, serving as a dark canvas upon which vivid pastel emissions—dreamy lavender, warm glowing amber, neon pink, and celestial cyan—disperse softly into the surroundings. Rather than adopting an aggressive cyber-noir tone, the UI maintains an intimate, restful, and artisanal warmth through rounded corner geometry, gentle chromatic glows, and pixel-stepped precision accents.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is anchored entirely in an ambient nighttime bedroom setting:

- **Primary (`#C084FC` - Luminous Lavender):** The central emissive signal. Represents core interactive triggers, focused selections, active navigational nodes, and bedside atmospheric glow.
- **Secondary (`#F472B6` - Soft Neon Pink):** Provides warm emotional contrast for celebratory states, cart actions, dynamic interactive highlights, and secondary light modes.
- **Tertiary (`#FDE047` - Glowing Amber 2700K):** Directly replicates warm bedside lamp filaments and cozy bedtime illumination. Used for warmth indicators, star ratings, active power toggles, and stock alerts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0D0B14` to `#1B1827` - Obsidian & Purple Slate):** A tiered series of dark tones that replace dead monochrome blacks with deep midnight violets, preserving depth and preventing eye strain in low-light environments.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `celestial-cyan` (`#38BDF8`): Cool LED spectrum accent, technical readouts, and connectivity statuses.
  - `ambient-2700k` (`#FBBF24`): Incandescent warm-lamp highlight for filament temperature previews.
  - `text-primary` (`#F5F3FF`): Luminescent off-white with faint lavender tint for peak readability against dark slate.
  - `text-muted` (`#9CA3AF`): Softened slate for secondary metadata and technical labels.

## Typography

Typography strikes an intentional contrast between tactile, humanistic surfaces and digital pixel-matrix micro-readouts:

- **Headlines & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Smooth, rounded forms mimic the tactile quality of 3D-printed curves. Rendered in high-contrast luminescent off-white (`#F5F3FF`), it prevents eye fatigue across deep slate surfaces while retaining crisp geometric legibility.
- **Hardware & Pixel Accents (`Space Mono`):** Dedicated to technical metadata, 3D printing specs (e.g., `[ LAYER: 0.16MM ]`, `[ 16x16 PIXEL MATRIX ]`, `[ TEMP: 2700K ]`), SKU counters, and micro-badges. Set in uppercase with open tracking to emulate backlit LED clock displays.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry simulates structured shelving, nightstands, and modular showcase displays:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid capped at 1360px container width, with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `3rem` canvas margins to give backlit cards room to cast ambient light without visual collision.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins, allowing lamp cards to span as full-width or two-up modules.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card structures use tight increments (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to keep pixel badges compact, while section breaks expand to `space-xl` and higher to emphasize relaxed nocturnal rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on opaque shadows, depth is manifested through **Light Emittance, Semi-Transparent Obsidian Glass, and Chromatic Auras**:

- **Ground Base (`#0D0B14`):** The pitch-dark room backdrop.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#13111C`):** Base product containers and modules framed with a delicate border: `1px solid rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1B1827`):** Raised interactive cards, dropdowns, and sheet panels. Uses a subtle ambient perimeter glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Active Lamp Glow):** Activated modules, modals, and hovered lamp displays radiate light like a physical LED source: `0 0 30px -4px rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.35), 0 0 12px 0 rgba(253, 224, 71, 0.25), inset 0 0 0 1.5px rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.5)`.
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation bars and floating badges utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(19, 17, 28, 0.75)` with a glowing edge line `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system blends smooth ergonomic radii with discrete stepped pixel micro-geometry:

- **Standard Containers:** Cards, modal dialogs, and primary inputs feature a `0.5rem` base radius (`rounded-lg` at `1rem`), reflecting the rounded corners of fused filament 3D prints.
- **Stepped Pixel Accents:** Corner tabs, action tags, and status containers incorporate crisp 2-4px notched corners or stepped pixel borders, referencing 8-bit voxel lamps without compromising modern ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Light Up" / Add to Cart):** Background in glowing lavender (`#C084FC`) with obsidian text (`#0D0B14`, weight 700). Accented with a permanent soft ambient blur: `box-shadow: 0 0 18px rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.45)`. On hover, the luminance expands to `0 0 28px rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.7)` with an inner white light flash.
- **Secondary Action (Explore / Specs):** Elevated obsidian container (`#1B1827`) with `1px solid rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.3)` and `#F5F3FF` typography. On hover, background shifts to `#262237` with pink perimeter glow (`#F472B6`).
- **Pixel Micro-Button:** Rectangular, sharp or 2px notched corners, monospace 11px font with glowing bracket accents: `[ DEMO GLOW ]`.

### Cards & Product Modules
- **Base Canvas:** Constructed with `#13111C` dark purple-slate, surrounded by a 1px border (`#352F4C`).
- **Lamp Stage Viewport:** Inset container in obsidian black (`#09080E`) centered with a diffuse radial gradient mimicking lamp light cones (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(253, 224, 71, 0.18) 0%, rgba(13, 11, 20, 0) 70%)`).
- **Interactive State:** Hovering activates an external chromatic perimeter glow that transitions smoothly from lavender to warm amber.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pixel Spec Chips:** Monospace micro-badges framed by `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)` filled with `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)` and celestial cyan text.
- **Warmth & Mood Badges:** Pill-shaped capsules with glowing amber (`#FDE047`) dot indicators showing kelvin temperature (e.g., `● 2700K COZY WARMTH`).

### Inputs, Checkboxes & Toggles
- **Form Inputs:** Filled with `#1B1827`, bordered by `#352F4C`, with placeholder text in `#9CA3AF`. On focus, the field casts a glowing ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.25)` and border `#C084FC`).
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with a 2px radius, dark slate fill, and a 2px chunky pixel checkmark in `#FDE047` that shines when checked.
- **Nightstand Power Toggle (Custom Component):** A bedside rocker switch toggling 3D lamp states online. Styled with a matte bioplastic slider track and an illuminated neon LED pip that switches between muted gray (`OFF`) and intense glowing amber (`ON`).
