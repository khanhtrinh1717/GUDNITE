[ Answers & Specs ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our materials, craft, and care.

What materials are used to make GUDNITE lamps? expand_more

All GUDNITE lamps are additive-manufactured using high-grade, bio-based PLA (Poly-Lactic Acid) derived from renewable fermented plant starch. It is completely odorless, non-toxic, and safe around children and pets.

What base color options are available? expand_more

You can pair any of our 4 signature silhouettes with 6 curated base colorways: Lavender Mist, Pastel Pink, Sky Blue, Mint Green, Butter Cream, and Obsidian Charcoal. The upper stepped pixel shade remains glowing warm white.

How fast is production and delivery? expand_more

Because we operate our own 3D print laboratory in Saigon, orders ship out within 24–48 hours. Express domestic delivery in Vietnam takes 1–3 business days. Regional Southeast Asia shipping takes 4–7 days.

Does the lamp get warm or pose fire risks? expand_more

No. The internal LED module runs on low-voltage 5V DC consuming merely 2.5W. Even when left on continuously overnight, the outer surface remains completely cool to the touch.

How is the lamp powered? expand_more

Each unit comes with a durable 1.5m braided USB-C cable with an integrated in-line power switch. You can plug it into any phone wall charger, laptop, or portable power bank for cord-free desk placement.

What warranty policy is included? expand_more

Every GUDNITE lamp includes a comprehensive 12-month direct replacement warranty covering all LED modules and electrical components. If anything fails, we replace it free of charge.

Can I customize text engravings or shapes? expand_more

To preserve the optical diffusion balance and industrial 3D print perfection, we do not accept custom text engravings or bespoke shape alterations. Customers choose between our 4 silhouettes and 6 base hues.
