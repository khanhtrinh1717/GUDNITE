## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, tactile, and nostalgic yet high-end digital storefront for 3D-printed artisanal night lamps. It merges the warm physical charm of bedtime ambient lighting with an early-digital pixel sensibility. Rather than skewing toward harsh arcade nostalgia or aggressive retro-gaming tropes, the aesthetic is tailored for design-conscious Gen Z and Millennial interior enthusiasts who appreciate collectible desk objects, cozy lo-fi bedroom setups, and tactile home decor.

The design movement combines **Modern Tactile Minimalism** with **Micro-Pixel Accents and Ambient Glow**. Large surfaces remain uncluttered, crisp, and breathable, letting dimensional product renders pop with physical warmth. Whimsical pixel geometries are deployed strictly as intentional micro-accents—such as stepped container borders, pixel-bracketed product labels, and miniature status badges—while primary content maintains the fluid elegance of modern boutique lifestyle e-commerce. The overall tone is calming, comforting, creative, and undeniably collectible.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic sensation of looking at warm diffuse LED lamps casting pastel light onto clean bedroom surfaces at dusk. 

- **Primary (`#8B5CF6` / `#DDD6FE` tint):** Dreamy Lavender serves as the brand's cornerstone tone. Used for dominant action buttons, highlighted states, and glowing brand focal points.
- **Secondary (`#F472B6` / `#FCE7F3` tint):** Soft Sunset Coral provides emotional warmth, celebratory accents, cart notifications, and secondary interaction states.
- **Tertiary (`#FDE047` / `#FEF08A` & `#FFFBEB` tints):** Ambient Night-Glow Yellow replicates the physical illumination of a nightstand lamp. Used for warm container highlights, product illumination ratings, and mood badges.
- **Supportive Accents:** Sky Blue (`#38BDF8` / `#E0F2FE`) and Mint Aurora (`#34D399` / `#D1FAE5`) appear in feature badges, material certifications, and color-swatch selectors.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:** The root background is Porcelain Off-White (`#FAFAF9`), paired with Pure Card White (`#FFFFFF`) and warm muted borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Typography & Text Neutrals:** High-contrast Deep Indigo-Slate (`#1E1B4B`) anchors primary headings and essential checkout info, avoiding lifeless pure black. Secondary body copy rests comfortably at Deep Slate Slate (`#334155`).

Color transitions in components should mimic dimmable incandescent shifts, utilizing gentle alpha channels and layered tints rather than heavy opaque blocks.

## Typography

The typographical framework balances high-legibility modern e-commerce utility with technical pixel nostalgia.

- **Primary Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Carries all storytelling, editorial hero headlines, functional e-commerce flows, product specs, and body copy. Its gentle curves, geometric clarity, and warm counters mirror the smooth, rounded finish of matte 3D-printed bioplastics.
- **Display Pixel Mono (`Space Mono`):** Chosen as the disciplined digital accent. It evokes 8-bit digital clocks and LED readouts without sacrificing readability or international character rendering. Applied strictly to small badges, technical SKU data, pixel specs (e.g., `[ 16x16 GRID ]`), stock counts, and promotional pills. Never use this font for paragraphs or critical form inputs.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large headlines must remain snug and compact via negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to impart an editorial poster presence. Pixel tags are set in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to mimic discrete digital alphanumeric matrix displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on a responsive 12-column grid system paired with an intentional 4px/8px incremental spacing rhythm:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and generous outer margins of `3rem` (48px) up to a max-width container of `1360px`. Content enjoys unhurried breathing room, mimicking the calm environment of an art gallery or boutique showroom.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` (32px) outer margins. Cards dynamically wrap to 2-column or 3-column matrices.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer canvas margins. Product grids collapse into a high-density 2-column layout with horizontal snap-carousels for collections.

### Spacing Philosophy
Generous vertical spacing separates curated lifestyle sections, allowing product images to dominate visually. Internal component gaps follow strict modular multiples: `space-xs` (4px) for inline badge icons, `space-sm` (8px) for related form labels and inputs, `space-md` (16px) for item metadata, and `space-xl` (40px) between distinct card clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through the metaphor of light emission rather than heavy architectural drop shadows. Surfaces glow outwards rather than casting murky dark shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base off-white canvas (`#FAFAF9`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with a clean 1.5px border in `#E2E8F0` overlaid with a pastel perimeter shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** Dropdowns, quick-buy popovers, and sticky navigation bars receive a layered ambient dispersion: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Illuminated Lamp Glow Focus):** Active state for lamp preview cards, modal dialogues, and active cart drawers. Replicates a lit bedside fixture via an internal rim glow (`inset 0 0 0 1.5px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`) combined with a multi-step chromatic blur: `0 12px 40px -6px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2), 0 2px 10px 0 rgba(254, 240, 138, 0.3)`.
- **Glass Surfaces:** Glassmorphism is reserved strictly for sticky headers and image floating badges: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a hybrid shape strategy: **Rounded Modular Foundations** paired with **Stepped Micro-Pixel Corner Details**.

- **Base Radius:** Primary containers, interactive cards, and product viewports utilize Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for medium cards, and `1.5rem` for prominent hero banners), providing an ergonomic, touch-friendly, safe feel that matches 3D-printed curved edges.
- **Pixel-Notch Micro-Details:** Selected status chips, product tag containers, and CTA buttons feature 4px notched corners (mimicking an aliased corner step) or sharp 2px inner border offsets. This delivers visual rhythm reminiscent of pixel grid builds while preserving smooth, modern interaction ergonomics.
- **Color Swatches:** Product lamp glow swatches are geometric rounded squares (`rounded-md`, 8px) enclosed within a 2px offset ring when active.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy):** Deep Lavender background (`#8B5CF6`) with clean white text, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. Border-radius of `0.75rem` (12px) with a signature 2px bottom shadow offset (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #6D28D9`). On hover, the button subtly drops 2px with an active lilac glow (`0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action (Configure / Explore):** Off-white base with a crisp 1.5px border (`#DDD6FE`), text in `#1E1B4B`. Hover state brings a gentle coral-tinted fill (`#FCE7F3`) and lavender border.
- **Pixel Micro-Button:** Compact square or rectangular utility button using `Space Mono` 11px uppercase. Displays a stepped 2px corner mask and a crisp 1px border.

### Badges & Chips
- **Category & Collection Badges:** Soft pill containers (`rounded-full`) filled with pastel tints: Mint (`#D1FAE5` with `#065F46` text), Sky (`#E0F2FE` with `#0369A1` text), or Coral (`#FCE7F3` with `#9D174D` text).
- **Pixel Spec Tags:** Rectangular block badges with 0px or 2px radius, surrounded by monospaced brackets (e.g., `[ 5V USB-C ]`, `[ WARM GLOW: 2700K ]`), set in `Space Mono` at 11px.

### Product Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card body with `1rem` radius, subtle 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Image Viewport:** Inset container with a pale pastel background (alternating between `#F5F3FF`, `#FFFBEB`, and `#F0FDF4`) displaying the 3D-rendered lamp.
- **Interactive State:** Hovering initiates a soft lamp "turn-on" effect—a radial gradient glow pulses behind the 3D lamp model, and the card shadow expands into a lavender-yellow ambient aura.

### Swatch Selectors
- **Hue / Filament Pickers:** Grid of 32x32px squircle buttons (`rounded-lg`). Each swatch shows the physical 3D filament color with a warm glowing pip in the center indicating lamp temperature. The selected state triggers a stepped pixel double-ring border in `#8B5CF6`.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Floating label format, porcelain background, 1.5px border in `#E2E8F0`, transitioning to Lavender `#8B5CF6` with a soft focused aura (`0 0 0 4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Distinctive custom squares with a deliberate 2px chunky pixel checkmark icon. When active, filled with `#8B5CF6` and checked with `#FEF08A` (light yellow).

### Mood-Light Toggle Switch (Custom Component)
- An ambient toggle switch allowing users to view the entire e-commerce site under "Daylight Room Mode" (crisp light canvas) or "Nightstand Ambient Mode" (dimmed warm slate backdrop highlighting lamp light cones). Styled with a tactile physical toggle slider accented by an 8-bit sun/moon icon.
